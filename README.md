# ⚡️ MetaZap Pro

**The Ultimate Client-Side Photo Privacy & Metadata Toolkit**

MetaZap Pro is a lightning-fast, privacy-first web application designed to strip hidden EXIF data, GPS coordinates, and AI watermarks (C2PA tags) from your photos. Built for journalists, photographers, and privacy-conscious users, it ensures your data remains yours.

## 🔒 Why MetaZap Pro? (Zero-Server Architecture)
Most online EXIF removers upload your personal photos to their servers, process them, and send them back. **We don't.** 
MetaZap Pro runs 100% locally in your browser. Your photos never leave your device, guaranteeing absolute privacy and security.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Total Metadata Stripping:** Instantly remove camera models, exposure settings, timestamps, and software traces.
- **GPS Location Removal:** Erase geotags and coordinates to protect your physical location.
- **Broad Format Support:** Seamlessly works with JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC images.
- **Dark Mode Optimized:** A beautiful, responsive UI crafted with Tailwind CSS that respects your system preferences.
- **Lightning Fast:** Powered by Next.js App Router for instant load times and optimal web performance.
